About Me

Hi, I'm a software engineer passionate about technology and writing. This blog is where I share my thoughts, experiences, and technical knowledge.

I've extensively worked on an SDN product on the control plane, so I've built a solid understanding of networking concepts over the years. I also have a specialization in cloud and distributed systems from my Master's program, which shaped how I think about designing and scaling systems.

My main focus these days is backend platform work and infrastructure for large-scale systems—including handling workloads related to AI/ML for building and optimizing distributed training and inference pipelines. I enjoy digging into how things work under the hood and figuring out how to make systems more reliable, scalable, and efficient.

Unpopular opinion: I actually love debugging. I know a lot of folks loathe it (fair enough), but to me, it's one of the most fun and rewarding parts of the job. Maybe that's why I enjoy dealing with production issues—it's the scale and complexity that make them feel like puzzles waiting to be solved. There's something deeply satisfying about tracking down the root cause of a tough bug. It's like solving a mystery.
